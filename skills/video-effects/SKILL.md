# Video effects

The hard cut does most of the work. Every other technique needs a reason in
the story, the feeling or the music.

1. Find the technique in its family:
   - [Cuts, transitions, time and speed](cuts-time.md)
   - [Framing, camera moves, text and graphics](framing-text.md)
   - [Compositing, masks, colour and looks](compositing-colour.md)
   - [Sound, music and mixing](sound.md)
2. Check its flag. Timeless: use freely. Contextual: only when the story or
   music motivates it. Overused or dated: only with a one-line reason, given
   to the user.
3. Start from the numbers in the entry (frames, %, dB), then adjust by watching
   and listening.
4. Do it in the app the entry names. Command names and checks are in the
   craft-suite skill; confirm them against the installed app.
5. After each technique, frame-step the change, watch it at full speed, and
   listen once.

Work in this order: structure, timing, sound, graphics, colour, final checks.
Correct colour before any look; set loudness last.

Ask before adding a technique the plan did not include, unless the user asked
for that technique by name.
