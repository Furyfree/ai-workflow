# Cuts, transitions, time and speed

Flags: timeless (basic film grammar), contextual (fine when motivated, looks
like a template by default), overused (saturated now), dated (signals an era).
The App column names where to start; commands and known gaps are in the
craft-suite skill.

## Cuts and transitions

| Technique | Flag | Use for | Avoid | How | App |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hard cut | Timeless | Everything | A camera change under 30° on the same subject; it reads as a jump | Cut at the end of a thought, on a blink or on motion. If a cut bumps, move it 2–4 frames | FilmCraft |
| J and L cut | Timeless | Interviews, speeches over B-roll, tributes, entering a scene | — | Offset audio from picture: 6–24 frames for dialogue, 1–3 s for ambience leading in | FilmCraft |
| Match cut (shape, action, sound) | Timeless; shape matches overused in travel reels | Then-to-now in tributes, ring to hands, product shape rhymes | A link with no meaning | Lay the outgoing shot at 50 % opacity over the next, align the key shape, cut where they coincide; optional 4–8 frame dissolve. Plan it while shooting | FilmCraft |
| Cut on action | Timeless | Any movement; tutorials (hand reaches, then close-up) | Over-cutting one action | Cut during the motion; start the next shot 1–3 frames further into it. Have the subject repeat the action for each angle | FilmCraft |
| Jump cut | Timeless as a device; overused as the YouTube default | Talking heads, tutorials, process montages | Anything meant to feel seamless | Remove pauses over ~0.5 s; alternate framing 100 % and 110–120 % every 2nd–3rd cut; ~2-frame audio crossfade; keep pauses after key points | FilmCraft |
| Smash cut | Timeless; overused if repeated | Comedy, ads, shock | Solemn pieces | Build tension, cut on the peak with no transition, jump the sound by 10 dB or more, or to silence | FilmCraft |
| Cross-dissolve | Timeless when meaningful | Time passing, montage, tributes, weddings | Every cut; shots with very different brightness | 1–3 s; a 4–8 frame soft cut hides small mismatches | FilmCraft |
| Dip to black or white | Black timeless, white contextual | Chapter breaks, endings, before title cards; white for memories | Mid-scene or repeatedly | 0.5–2 s; hold black 6–24 frames on weighty endings; fade the audio with it | FilmCraft |
| Whip pan | Contextual; overused in travel | Energetic jumps in time or place on the music | Footage not shot for it | Shoot it in camera, same direction and speed both sides, slow shutter. Cut inside the blur; add a whoosh | FilmCraft |
| Zoom transition | Dated, overused | Sizzle reels, once or twice | Interviews, client work | Scale 100→300 % from 6 frames before to 6 after the cut, mirrored edges, motion blur at a 300–360° shutter | FilmCraft |
| Mask or object wipe | Contextual | Travel, events, wedding entrances, ads | Objects that don't cover the frame | Outgoing shot above, mask keyed along the passing object's edge, feathered; frame-step for slivers | FilmCraft, EffectCraft |
| Light leak or flash | Contextual; overused in weddings | Weddings, holidays, nostalgic tributes | Tutorials, corporate, news-style docs | Screen blend over the cut, brightest frame on the cut, 40–80 % opacity, 12–24 frames, tinted to the grade | FilmCraft |
| Glitch | Dated outside tech and music | Tech, gaming, music video | Weddings, tributes, docs | 4–12 frames total, synced to a glitch sound, clean frame on the beat | FilmCraft |
| Morph cut | Contextual utility | Hiding jump cuts in single-person interviews | Moving backgrounds, hands, head turns | Cut in a pause, 6–12 frames; fall back to a cutaway if it warps | FilmCraft |
| Hidden cut | Timeless | One-take walkthroughs, music video, ads | — | Cut on the darkest or blurriest frame as something blocks the lens; match camera speed and height; keep sound continuous | FilmCraft |

## Time and speed

| Technique | Flag | Use for | Avoid | How | App |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speed ramp | Contextual; overused if every clip ramps | Sports, action, travel, ads, dance floors | Interviews; low-frame-rate footage | Eased speed curve over 4–12 frames, slowest point on the impact or beat, optical flow. Shoot 60 fps or more | FilmCraft, EffectCraft |
| Slow motion | Timeless | Rings, first dance, sports, product hero shots | Lip-synced dialogue | Conform by ratio: in a 25 fps timeline, 50 fps = 50 %, 100 fps = 25 %. Shutter 1/(2 × capture rate). Frame blending ghosts; optical flow warps on crossing objects | FilmCraft |
| Timelapse or hyperlapse | Timelapse timeless, hyperlapse contextual | Sunsets, set-ups, cities | Auto exposure | Shutter about half the interval (ND filter); at least 250 frames for 10 s at 25 fps; hyperlapse keeps one anchor point and even steps, then stabilise and deflicker | LightCraft, EffectCraft |
| Freeze frame | Contextual; the "record scratch, that's me" freeze is overused | Introducing people, comic beats, a tribute's last frame | Blurry or eyes-closed frames | Hold, then a 100→105 % push, an impact sound, slight desaturation, a name card | FilmCraft |
| Reverse, rewind | Reverse contextual, VHS rewind dated | Music video, kids' "magic", intros | Factual docs; audible reversed speech | Rewind: duplicate the section, reverse the middle copy at 300 %, add a rewind sound | FilmCraft |
| Stop-motion feel | Contextual | Unboxing, crafts, titles, kids | Over camera moves (judder) | Posterize time at 12 fps in a 24–25 fps timeline, plus slight irregular shake | FilmCraft |
| Beat-synced cutting | Timeless; cutting on every downbeat is overused | Music video, wedding highlights, travel, ads, recaps | Cutting only on bar lines | Mark beats; a cut on the sixteenth before a downbeat feels livelier; verse about one cut per 2 bars, chorus every 1 or ½ bar; then move some cuts off the beat for feeling | SoundCraft, FilmCraft |
