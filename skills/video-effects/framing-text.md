# Framing, camera moves, text and graphics

Move the frame only when the subject or the story motivates it. Text must stay
on screen long enough to read twice, inside safe areas, never over a face.
The App column names where to start; commands and known gaps are in the
craft-suite skill.

## Framing and camera moves in post

| Technique | Flag | Use for | Avoid | How | App |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Punch-in zoom | Timeless; overused on every sentence | Talking heads, tutorial emphasis, product close-ups | Zooming past 100 % of the source; centred zooms on off-centre subjects | 4K in a 1080 timeline; alternate 100 % and 110–120 %; ease the keyframes | FilmCraft |
| Ken Burns on photos | Timeless | Tributes, docs, family history | The same direction on every photo | Slow 100→120 % with eased ends, 5–8 s per photo, toward the eyes or along the gaze; photos at 1.5–2× output resolution | FilmCraft |
| 2.5D parallax | Contextual; overused on every photo | Key photos in tributes and docs | Photos where lines join foreground and background | Split the photo into layers and fill behind them; spread the layers in 3D depth with a 35 mm camera and an eased move | PhotoCraft, EffectCraft |
| Digital push or pan | Contextual | Life in locked-off interviews and B-roll | Moving against the subject; moving every shot | 2–5 % drift over the shot; a UHD source allows up to 2× in an HD timeline | FilmCraft |
| Reframe 16:9 to 9:16 | Timeless need | Every vertical version | Fast motion and two-subject shots without manual fixes | Auto reframe, then fix keyframes; faces near the vertical centre, clear of top labels and the right-hand buttons | FilmCraft, EffectCraft |
| Stabilisation | Timeless repair | Handheld family, travel and event footage | Removing handheld energy you want; speed-changed clips | Start at 10–30 % smoothness; switch method if faces or buildings wobble; auto-scale under ~115 % | EffectCraft, FilmCraft |
| Added shake | Contextual | Impacts, music drops, matching a locked shot to handheld | Tutorials, interviews | Wiggle on position (about 1 Hz, 25 px), slight rotation, scale up to hide edges; impact jolts decay over 3–6 frames | FilmCraft, EffectCraft |
| Split screen | Timeless | Before and after, comparisons, simultaneous action | Too many panels | Crop or mask each panel, 0–8 px gutter, eyelines facing each other, slide in over 8–12 frames | FilmCraft |
| Picture-in-picture | Timeless utility | Face cam over screen recordings, reactions, presentations | Covering important UI; jumping corners | 25–35 % scale inside the safe area, 2–4 px border, soft shadow at 20–40 % | FilmCraft |
| Letterbox | Contextual; fake bars on vertical video are overused | Short films, wedding trailers, music video | Tutorials, presentations, vertical social | 2.39:1 is about 12 % cropped top and bottom; never mix ratios in one piece | FilmCraft |

## Text, titles and graphics

| Technique | Flag | Use for | Avoid | How | App |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lower thirds | Timeless | Interviews, docs, events, speeches | Spinning or bouncing entries; covering faces | Hold 4–6 s, enter 1–2 s after the speaker starts, 10–15 frame eased in and out; at 1080p name ~45–60 px, title ~32–40 px | FilmCraft, EffectCraft |
| Kinetic typography | Contextual; animating every word is overused | Explainers, ads, lyric videos | Too fast to read; competing effects | One motion idea per piece; sharp fast motion feels urgent, slow eased motion calm; key words land 0–2 frames before the voice | EffectCraft |
| Sentence subtitles | Timeless | Docs, weddings, tutorials, anything with speech | All caps (slower to read) | At most 42 characters per line and 2 lines, at most 20 characters per second, 0.83–7 s each; white with a 2 px outline or a 70–80 % black box | FilmCraft |
| Word-by-word captions | Overused on social, useful for sound-off viewing | Reels, Shorts, TikTok, ads | Weddings, tributes, docs | 1–4 words per card, active word highlighted, switched on the syllable; in 9:16 placed 55–75 % down the frame | FilmCraft, EffectCraft |
| Title cards | Timeless | Chapters, names and date, event titles | Too many fonts; too short to read | Under about a third of frame width, over open areas, held 2–4 s; a slight tracking increase for life | FilmCraft |
| End cards | Timeless | YouTube end screens, ads, tributes | Elements over faces | YouTube: last 5–20 s of a video of at least 25 s, up to 4 elements. Ads: logo, call to action and URL for 2–4 s | FilmCraft |
| Callouts and arrows | Timeless | Tutorials, product demos, explainers, sports | Untracked arrows on moving things; clutter | Stroke drawn on over 8–12 frames, 4–8 px at 1080, one accent colour, at most 2 on screen | FilmCraft, EffectCraft |
| Map or route animation | Timeless | Holiday films, travel, docs | Uneven draw speed | Route as its own vector, drawn on with trim paths, a vehicle auto-oriented along it, labels popping in over ~12 frames; 3–6 s per leg | VectorCraft, EffectCraft |
| Counters and data graphics | Timeless | Explainers, fundraising, sports, product stats | Truncated axes; no source | Count over 1–2 s with ease-out; tabular figures; cite the source on screen | EffectCraft |
| Logo animation | Timeless craft; template particle reveals overused | Intros, outros, ads | Long intros; distorting the logo | 2–4 s for intros, 0.5–1 s for social; anticipation, overlap and easing; a sound hit on the resolve | EffectCraft |
| Type pairing | Timeless | Every project | Decorative fonts for captions | At most 2 families: a clean sans for captions, an optional display or serif for titles | — |
| Safe areas | Timeless | Every project | Trusting the frame edge | Action safe 90 %, title safe 80 %; 9:16 keeps the top 14 %, bottom 35 % and 6 % of each side clear | — |
