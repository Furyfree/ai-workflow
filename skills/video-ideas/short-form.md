# Short-form: Reels, TikTok, Shorts

Five rules carry most of it: picture and sound both hook in the first 1–3 s,
design for sound on but make it work muted, keep text inside the safe zone,
test different concepts rather than near-copies, and never use commercial
music in an ad. Platform numbers change often; recheck them on the platform's
help pages before relying on them.

## Platforms

| | Instagram and Facebook Reels | TikTok | YouTube Shorts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Format | 9:16, 1080×1920, H.264 and AAC | 9:16, at least 720p | Vertical or square; 16:9 is treated as long-form |
| Length | 15–60 s typical; uploads can be longer | 15–60 s typical | Up to 3 min |
| Safe zone | Top 14 %, bottom 35 %, sides 6 %; right-hand buttons take more | About 220 / 640 / 120 / 240 px top, bottom, left, right at 1080×1920 | About 180 px top, 390 bottom, 120 right, 60 left |
| Music | Business accounts get only Meta's Sound Collection; boosting a Reel with licensed music forces a swap | Commercial Music Library for business use | Over 1 min with any active Content ID claim is blocked |
| Loudness | No official target; −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP in practice | Same | −14 LUFS, turned down only |

The strictest common text area is 720×1200 px, from y = 220 to y = 1420.

## Formats

- Talking head: one point per video, jump cuts with punch-ins, captions.
- Faceless explainer: voice-over, B-roll that follows the script, captions,
  quiet music (full flow in [recipes.md](recipes.md)).
- List: "3 things …", each item its own beat with a number on screen.
- 60-second tutorial: show the result first, then the steps at speed.
- Before and after: before 0–8 s, after 8–20 s, how 20–27 s, call to action.
- POV or day-in-the-life: first-person shots, natural sound, few words.
- Recap: the best moments of an event or trip, cut to the music.
- Cutdown: a 30–60 s piece taken from a long video, re-hooked for vertical.

## Structure

- Hook in 0–3 s. Three hook types: a value promise, a statement of intent, a
  question. Picture and sound together; movement in the first frame; text on
  screen within half a second; no logo or slow intro.
- Payoff: deliver what the hook promised, one idea only.
- End on a loop back to the start, or a call to action held at least 3 s.
- Ads (30 s): problem 0–5 → agitate 5–18 → solution 18–25 → call to action;
  or before → after → bridge; or a testimonial: personal hook, context,
  discovery, proof, recommendation.

## Cutting and captions

- Cut every 1.5–3 s, faster in the hook; a visual change at least every 3 s.
- Word-by-word captions: 1–4 words per card, the active word highlighted, large
  and centred 55–75 % down the frame, white with a dark outline.
- Over 80 % of Reels play with sound, so design for sound on, but put the key
  claim on screen too; messages carried by both picture and sound perform better.
- Reframe 16:9 to 9:16 with tracking, faces near the vertical centre; never
  letterbox horizontal footage.

## Testing and originality

- Make variants that differ in concept or hook, not just colour. Meta: at most
  6 ads per ad set; TikTok: 3–5 creatives per ad group.
- Hook rate = 3-second views ÷ impressions (about 20–25 % is normal, over 30 %
  strong). Hold rate = completed views ÷ 3-second views (about 40–50 %).
- Instagram stops recommending accounts that mostly repost others' content;
  never export with another platform's watermark.
- YouTube's monetisation rules target mass-produced, repetitive "inauthentic"
  content, and Meta is moving the same way. Every video needs its own idea,
  footage choices and edit; never batch near-identical videos.
