# Video ideas

Plan before editing. Nothing is cut, rendered or downloaded before the user
approves the plan.

1. **Brief.** Ask, one question at a time, for what the request leaves open:
   purpose and the one message, audience, length and platforms, deadline, a
   reference video, music rights, which footage exists, consent. Checklist in
   [kit.md](kit.md).
2. **Recipe.** Find the video type in [recipes.md](recipes.md). For Reels,
   TikTok and Shorts also read [short-form.md](short-form.md).
3. **Three concepts.** Write three one-paragraph concepts that differ in
   structure, opening hook, music and ending. Drop the most obvious one and
   recommend one.
4. **Plan.** For the chosen concept: beats with timings, shot list or selects,
   music (mood, BPM, licensed source), text and graphics, the look, and the
   taste dials from [process.md](process.md). Name any banned default it uses
   and why.
5. **Wait for approval.** Post the plan, then stop.
6. **Build in passes** with video-effects and craft-suite: structure, timing,
   sound, graphics, colour, checks. Show the structure pass before polishing.
7. **Final check** from [process.md](process.md) before calling it done.
   Report what passed and what could not be checked.

With a reference video: measure it as [process.md](process.md) describes and
copy its proportions, never its content. To watch an online reference, use
video-analysis.
