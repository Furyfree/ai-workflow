# Creative process

Creativity comes from process more than effects. Murch's priority order decides
every cut: emotion (51 %), story (23 %), rhythm (10 %), eye-trace (7 %), 2D
plane (5 %), 3D space (4 %). When something must give, sacrifice from the
bottom up.

## Method

1. Three concepts. Before cutting, write three one-paragraph concepts that
   differ in structure (chronological, by theme, or starting at the peak),
   song, opening hook and ending. Pick by fit to the brief and the feeling.
2. Constraints. Pick two or three per project, such as: cuts and one
   transition type only, two fonts, no speed ramps, music without voice-over,
   under 60 s, one accent colour.
3. Reference. Measure a video the user likes instead of eyeballing it: shot
   lengths and cuts per 30 s over time, the share of wide, medium and close
   shots, music level against dialogue, transitions by type, text and sound
   effects per minute, a few palette frames. Copy the proportions, never the
   content.
4. Storyboard or animatic for ads, explainers and anything with VFX: rough
   frames cut to a scratch voice at the target length. Derive the shot list from
   what the edit needs: inserts, repeated actions, room tone, clean plates,
   cutaways.
5. Paper edit for interviews, docs and speeches: order transcript quotes with
   timecodes, note B-roll and music, cut tangents, then build an audio-only
   "radio edit" and play it back.
6. Passes: structure with no effects, then trim 10–20 % and fix timing, then
   sound, graphics, colour, and technical checks. No polish before the
   structure is approved.
7. Pacing: hook fast, settle, build, peak, breathe before the end. Vary the
   median shot length by 30–50 % between sections. Keep the subject in a
   similar screen position across fast cuts.
8. Stop when the brief is met, two viewings in a row show no bumps, changes
   have become swaps rather than improvements, and the checks pass. Keep the
   last ~10 % of the time for checking.

## Banned by default

Each needs a one-line reason tied to feeling or story before it is used:

- Zoom transitions, light leaks or a whoosh on every cut; dissolves everywhere
- Teal and orange, or any creative LUT, as the default look
- Cutting only on bar lines; repeated smash cuts; over-cutting one action
- Glitch outside tech or music; fake letterbox on vertical video
- Word-by-word captions on weddings and tributes; "record scratch" freeze frames
- Trailer "braams" in family films; logo intros over 5 s
- Heavy vignettes; maxed grain and halation
- The same Ken Burns direction on every photo; skies whose light contradicts
  the shadows

## Taste dials

Set each from 0 to 10 in the plan: pace (median shot length), how visible
transitions are, graphics density, how stylised the grade is, texture (grain
and halation), sound effects per minute, music level against speech, camera
motion added in post.

## Genre matrix

| Genre | Median shot | Favour | Avoid | Look | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family or holiday | 2–4 s | J and L cuts, beat montage, map routes, timelapse, slow motion on moments | Glitch, zoom transitions | Natural, light grain | Music plus natural sound |
| Wedding | 2–5 s | Vows over B-roll, slow motion, a few dissolves, one dip to white | Glitch, a whoosh per cut, word-by-word captions | Warm, skin first, subtle film look | Silence before key lines |
| Event or conference | 1.5–3 s | Beat cuts, light ramps, set-up hyperlapse, lower thirds | Heavy looks | Clean, cameras matched | Crowd ambience, ducked music |
| Tribute | 4–8 s | Ken Burns or parallax, dissolves, then-to-now match cuts, black and white, narration over photos | Sound hits, fast pace | Gentle | Room tone, silence |
| Tutorial | 3–8 s | Punch-ins, callouts, picture-in-picture, before and after | Decorative transitions | Accurate, neutral | Clean dialogue, −14 LUFS |
| Explainer | Voice-driven | Kinetic type, counters, maps, track mattes | Live-action clichés | Brand palette | Voice-led, light effects |
| Product | 1–3 s | Macro slow motion, ramps, shape matches, clean plates | Heavy grain | Accurate colour | Foley clicks |
| Ad | 0.7–2 s | Strong first beat, smash cuts, beat sync, logo and call-to-action end card | Long intros | Brand look | −14 LUFS, risers into hits |
| Short-form social | 1.5–3 s | Hook in 0–3 s, word-by-word captions, punch-ins, loops | Slow starts, letterboxing | Clean, bright | Sound on, but works muted |
| Interview or doc | 4–10 s | Paper edit, J and L cuts, cutaways, sound bridges | Stylised transitions, teal and orange | Natural | Dialogue chain, room tone |
| Presentation | 3–6 s | Picture-in-picture, lower thirds, data graphics, morph cut | Glitch, leaks | Clean | Ducked music |
| Music video | Free | Anything motivated: off-beat variety, split screen, stop-motion, reverse, film look | Lip-sync drift | Stylised | The song |

## Final check

- One full viewing for bumps, one muted, one with audio only
- Loudness and true peak on target
- Subtitles proofread, under 20 characters per second, inside safe areas
- Skin on the vectorscope line in every shot; no clipped highlights
- Slowed and morph-cut sections rendered and checked frame by frame
- Vertical versions checked against the platform's buttons and labels
- Music and stock licences recorded with the project
